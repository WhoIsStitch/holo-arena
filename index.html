How to play

  • Win by taking all 6 Prize cards, leaving your opponent with no Pokémon in play, or when they can't draw at the start of their turn.
  • Your turn: draw, then do any of: bench Basic Pokémon, evolve, attach 1 Energy, play Items (unlimited), 1 Supporter, 1 Stadium, retreat once, use Abilities — then attack (or end turn).
  • Controls: hover a card to inspect it. Click (or tap) a hand card to play it, or drag it onto a Pokémon. Click/tap your Pokémon to attack, use abilities or retreat. On phones, tapping any card opens a bigger view with its actions.
  • The first player can't attack or play a Supporter on turn 1, and nobody evolves on their first turn.
  • Online: one player taps Host a room and shares the 5-character code (or the invite link); the other types it under Join. Works on phones and computers, peer-to-peer, no accounts.
