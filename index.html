How to play

  • Win by taking all 6 Prize cards, leaving your opponent with no Pokémon in play, or when they can't draw at the start of their turn.
  • Your turn: draw, then do any of: bench Basic Pokémon, evolve, attach 1 Energy, play Items (unlimited), 1 Supporter, 1 Stadium, retreat once, use Abilities — then attack (or end your turn).
  • Controls: tap (or click) any card to float it up. Your cards show buttons: Play, Attach to…, Evolve, Attack, Ability, Retreat. You can also drag a hand card onto a Pokémon.
  • The first player can't attack or play a Supporter on turn 1, and nobody evolves on their first turn.
  • Online: one player taps Host a room and shares the 5-character code (or the invite link); the other types it under Join. Peer-to-peer, no accounts.
